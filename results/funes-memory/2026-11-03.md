## funes

funes reads the published memory, so its hits are in the memory's numbering.

- `score_recall` (funes's `benchmark/score_recall.rs`) asked the questions at recall's defaults. Its
  output is [funes/score_recall.txt](funes/score_recall.txt):

  ```
  funes 1.10.0+dev, memory hf://datasets/huggingface/funes-memory @ 347686a1c7ed495fb653d650d78f9b92f8744c7a, questions /home/ubuntu/funes-recall-benchmark/funes-memory/questions.jsonl sha256 8f7042fa389e8af6946ee39f6155d8145a23fe3d99a145cd01dd5edfcd56a72e
  244 questions, 8 hits a question from a pool of 30 reranked, p50 7156 ms

                   hit@1 hit@5 found@8    MRR
  all                 62   111     128  0.339   (of 244)
    decision          14    27      37  0.257   (of 77)
    investigation     29    47      50  0.433   (of 85)
    verdict           19    37      41  0.318   (of 82)
  ```

  Its median latency comes from a machine running other tools at the same time.
- `drivers/funes.py` asked them again through `funes recall`, as an agent does, for the hits
  themselves: [funes/recall.jsonl](funes/recall.jsonl). It read a local copy of the memory at the same
  revision, at a path no session mentions, so that a hit's `→ get` line is told from one quoted inside
  another hit's text. It returned 8 hits for every question and ranks every question as
  `score_recall` does, so funes's rows come from its hits.
- funes and `score_recall` were built from `huggingface/funes` at `29075cb`.

The control: funes indexed the rebuilt transcripts the other tools read, and `score_recall` asked the
same questions of that index.

- Each file was converted with funes's Claude Code converter (`claude/claude-plugin/funes/convert` of
  `huggingface/funes-integrations` at `82bfebe`) and indexed with `funes index <dir> --yes` in a
  `FUNES_HOME` of its own: 139 sessions, 72,426 chunks.
- `tools/turnmap.py gold` moved each gold span to the files' numbering (one turn a line).
- It finds the same 128 answers (hit@1 65, hit@5 114, MRR 0.351). 215 questions keep their rank, 17
  rank higher and 12 lower (sign test p = 0.46), and 6 answers swap each way. Its output is
  [funes/score_recall-rebuilt.txt](funes/score_recall-rebuilt.txt).

So the rebuild does not move funes's score, and the other tools' rows read the files funes would.

## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents:
  24,207 drawers from the 139 files. The `mcp-sweep` arm copies that palace and adds
  `mempalace sweep ~/.claude/projects/`, one drawer per record (39,214).
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default
  limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU.
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's own normalizer and chunker on each file, keeping each
  record's offsets. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 139 of 139 files, and 24,207
  of 24,207 chunks equal their stored drawer (4 once a NUL is read as U+FFFD, as the store keeps it).
  A drawer is the run of turns its text covers: median 1 turn per passage returned, at most 19. A
  swept drawer holds its record's uuid. All 1,952 passages of each arm were placed this way. Scored
  at each drawer's main turn only, `mcp` gives 82 instead of 85.
- Convo mining drops thinking blocks and Read, Edit and Write results, keeps the first and last 20
  lines of a Bash output and 2 KB of other results, and cuts a tool call to one line. The first line
  of 31 gold quotes is not in its transcript, though every gold span keeps a record it holds text for.
- The saved index held 23,553 of the 24,207 drawers, and one question's top 8 changed across server
  starts. Four runs of each arm gave the same hit@1, hit@5 and found@8.

## agentmemory 0.9.31

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine v0.25.0 its CLI pins. `agentmemory`
  starts the server, `agentmemory import-jsonl` (its default root, under its default cap of 200
  files) imports 139 files as 38,934 observations, one per content block. The 401 records that hold
  only thinking get none.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to:
  `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10
  results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a
  hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 2,440 of 2,440 passages.
- Keyless indexing keeps a title, a 120-character subtitle and a 400-character narrative per block,
  and its ranking keeps at most 3 passages per session before it fills the rest.
  `memory_smart_search` also lists 10 lessons per question, which name no session and are not scored.

## deja-vu 0.23.5

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 139 sessions, 23,597 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the
  hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (1,174 passages), else by its text in
  the session (2,221, and 295 of those in several turns, placed at the first).
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its
  page shows 2 or 3 sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names
  (1,784 at one turn, 260 at several, 52 not found and counted for the session row only).
- deja-vu scores itself by sessions: on its own day-zero page, over LongMemEval chats. Its session row
  here is 171 (`search`).

## ctx 2.3.0

- `ctx setup --wait`, ctx's documented way of reading existing history: 139 sessions, 38,934 events.
  Analytics and upgrade checks off.
- `search`: `ctx search --format json -- <q>` at its defaults (lexical, 20 results, one event per
  session). Its MCP `search` tool returns the same events in the same order (checked on 10
  questions), and `ctx search -- <q>` lists them in the same order for 244 of 244.
- Placement: a result carries its record's timestamp to the millisecond (4,880 of 4,880 placed by it)
  and the record's line in `event_seq`, which agrees for all of them.
- One event per session means the 8 passages come from 8 sessions. For 76 questions the gold session
  is among them but its event is outside the span. Its MCP tool's text shows only the first 5.
- `hybrid`: the local E5 model, after unpacking the ONNX Runtime ctx ships apart from the binary
  (`ctx-onnxruntime-linux-x64.tar.gz` of the same release) where its installer puts it, and with its
  embedding throttle off. It embedded 2,150 messages. A semantic passage is placed at the messages it
  cites, a run when it cites several.

## cass 0.10.0

- `cass index --full --json`: 139 conversations, 38,813 messages. `cass search <q> --robot --limit 8`
  (its default limit returns every match). A hit carries its message's own timestamp, which places
  it at a turn (every passage of every arm).
- Its lexical query needs every word of the question in one message: 178 of 244 questions get no
  hit. `hybrid` is the same command after `cass models install --yes` and `cass index --semantic`,
  when its default mode fuses lexical and MiniLM results.
- It indexes a tool call as `[Tool: name - description]` and leaves thinking out.

## claude-history 0.1.81

- `claude-history agent search <q>`, the interface its Claude Code skill gives an agent, run from a
  directory that is no project of the corpus. Its first search builds the keyword store.
  `--generate-semantic-cache` embeds every conversation with its local model for `--mode semantic`
  and `--mode hybrid`, the two modes its skill gives for conceptual recall.
- Its default 6,000-character output budget shows about 5 passages a question in semantic mode, so
  the `-nobudget` arms ask again with `--no-budget` for 8.
- Placement: a hit names message ordinals (`focus=mA..mB`), placed at the file lines `agent read`
  gives for them. Lexical hits are single messages. Semantic hits are often runs (median 11 turns, at
  most 225), which overlap a ±3 span easily, so each is also scored at the turn of the run that holds
  its preview (`.narrowed`). In `semantic`, the preview is in a turn of 1,185 of the 1,214 placed
  passages (805 of them runs). The narrowed rows are the lower reading, the whole runs the upper one.
- Its lexical mode needs every word somewhere in a conversation, then ranks its messages.

## agentsview 0.46.1

- `AGENTSVIEW_NO_DAEMON=1 agentsview sync`: 139 sessions, 24,367 messages. Searches go through its
  daemon, stopped at the end. Telemetry and update checks off.
- `default`: `agentsview session search <q> --json`, a substring match: none of the 244 questions
  appears as typed. `fts` adds `--fts`, which needs every term in one message.
- Search leaves out one-shot sessions by default (15 here, holding the gold of 24 questions).
  `fts-keywords-oneshot` adds `--include-one-shot`.
- Placement: each match carries its message's timestamp, which places it (every passage).
