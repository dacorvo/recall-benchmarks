## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents: 24,207 drawers from 139 of the 139 files.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU, from Python 3.12.16 with the packages `uv` resolved on 2026-11-10 (in the bucket's run record).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 139 of the 139 files with drawers, and 24,207 of their 24,207 drawers equal the stored text (4 once a NUL is read as U+FFFD, as the store keeps it). All 1,920 passages came from those drawers and were placed at the run of turns each covers: median 1 turn, at most 19.
- `mcp-sweep` copies that palace and adds `mempalace sweep ~/.claude/projects/`, one drawer per record (39,214). Its passages were placed the same way.

## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server, and `agentmemory import-jsonl` (its default root, under its default cap of 200 files) imports 139 files as 38,934 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 2,400 of 2,400 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 139 sessions, 23,597 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (1,177 passages), else by its text in the session (2,441, and 284 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 12 were not found and count for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (1,775 at one turn, 268 at several, 35 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
