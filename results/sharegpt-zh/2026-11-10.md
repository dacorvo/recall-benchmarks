## Results

Ranks among the first 8 passages, scored by `tools/score.py`. A question is found when a passage shows one of its answering turns, as its turn, as a turn of its run, or in the frame funes shows beside a hit. `as a hit` counts the questions where a passage is an answering turn itself. `session` is found@8 counting a passage's session alone.

## funes

## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents: 7,200 drawers from 1,983 of the 2,000 files.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU, from Python 3.12.16 with the packages `uv` resolved on 2026-11-10 (in the bucket's run record).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 1,982 of the 1,983 files with drawers, and 7,181 of their 7,181 drawers equal the stored text. All 800 passages came from those drawers and were placed at the run of turns each covers: median 2 turns, at most 4.

## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server. `agentmemory import-jsonl` reads 200 files by default, so the driver imported each project directory in batches of up to 1,000 files with `--max-files 1000`: 2 imports, 2,000 files as 6,738 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 1,000 of 1,000 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 2,000 sessions, 6,738 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (1,306 passages), else by its text in the session (116, and 10 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 3 were not found and count for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (268 at one turn, 3 at several, 12 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
