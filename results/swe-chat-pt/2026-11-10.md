## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents: 19,692 drawers from 153 of the 153 files.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU, from Python 3.12.16 with the packages `uv` resolved on 2026-11-10 (in the bucket's run record).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 153 of the 153 files with drawers, and 19,692 of their 19,692 drawers equal the stored text. All 288 passages came from those drawers and were placed at the run of turns each covers: median 1 turn, at most 14.

## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server, and `agentmemory import-jsonl` (its default root, under its default cap of 200 files) imports 153 files as 37,814 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 360 of 360 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 153 sessions, 21,781 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (130 passages), else by its text in the session (395, and 85 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 3 were not found and count for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (214 at one turn, 37 at several, 3 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
