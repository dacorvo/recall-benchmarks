## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents: 135,358 drawers from 714 of the 715 files.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU, from Python 3.12.16 with the packages `uv` resolved on 2026-11-10 (in the bucket's run record).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 672 of the 714 files with drawers, and 135,307 of their 135,307 drawers equal the stored text (7 once a NUL is read as U+FFFD, as the store keeps it). All 800 passages came from those drawers and were placed at the run of turns each covers: median 1 turn, at most 8.

## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server. `agentmemory import-jsonl` reads 200 files by default, so the driver imported each project directory in batches of up to 1,000 files with `--max-files 1000`: 59 imports, 715 files as 177,361 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 1,000 of 1,000 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 715 sessions, 97,151 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (441 passages), else by its text in the session (1,134, and 292 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 5 were not found and count for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (796 at one turn, 160 at several, 18 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
