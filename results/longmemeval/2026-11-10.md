## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server. `agentmemory import-jsonl` reads 200 files by default, so the driver imported each project directory in batches of up to 1,000 files with `--max-files 1000`: 20 imports, 19,195 files as 199,499 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 4,700 of 4,700 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 19,195 sessions, 199,499 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (7,029 passages), else by its text in the session (47, and 3 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 1 was not found and counts for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (4,472 at one turn, 53 at several, 52 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
