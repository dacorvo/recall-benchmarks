  `98d7416c`. A gold span is an evidence turn and the turn after it, in its session or a copy of it

## Copies of a gold session

731 of the 19,195 sessions repeat the non-blank turns of another session under a different id
([longmemeval/](../../longmemeval/)). 318 of the 837 sessions LongMemEval marks have copies, and the
gold names the same spans in those copies. Scored on the marked spans alone (`questions.jsonl` at
`ef38654`, sha256 `6d291fc7…`), every arm finds as many or fewer:

| tool | arm | hit@1 | hit@5 | found@8 | MRR | session |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| funes | `recall` | 120 → 148 | 239 → 239 | 252 → 254 | 0.358 → 0.393 | 279 → 280 |
| agentmemory | `smart` | 88 → 105 | 197 → 198 | 223 → 225 | 0.282 → 0.306 | 274 → 276 |
| deja-vu | `search` | 102 → 123 | 187 → 199 | 212 → 219 | 0.287 → 0.325 | 273 → 280 |
| deja-vu | `recall` | 101 → 120 | 136 → 161 | 158 → 184 | 0.252 → 0.298 | 200 → 219 |
| MemPalace | `mcp` | 74 → 94 | 118 → 141 | 129 → 153 | 0.194 → 0.240 | 165 → 194 |
| MemPalace | `mcp-sweep` | 65 → 81 | 124 → 144 | 143 → 161 | 0.193 → 0.229 | 181 → 200 |

A copy's turn holds the same text as the marked one, so a tool scores the two alike and returns
either first. funes's hit@1 gains 28 this way. MemPalace's `mcp` keeps one copy of each conversation,
and for 171 marked sessions the one it kept is the copy, so on the marked spans alone it could not
find them.

- `drivers/funes.py` asked them again through `funes recall`, as an agent does, for the hits
  themselves: [funes/recall.jsonl](funes/recall.jsonl). It read a local copy of the memory at the same
  revision, at a path no session mentions, so that a hit's `→ get` line is told from one quoted inside
  another hit's text. It returned 8 hits for every question, and funes's rows come from these hits.
- The two runs rank 468 of 470 questions alike. In the 2 others, a gold turn's text also opens
  sessions that are not copies of the gold session (b320f3f8's first turn is also the first turn of
  `3bc55308` and `f26d40a1`). Those turns get the same score, and the two runs ordered them
  differently. 12 questions have an evidence turn whose text is also in a session outside their gold.
- An earlier `score_recall` run, scored on the marked spans alone (the questions file before copies),
  found 253 (hit@1 116, hit@5 238, MRR 0.354): [funes/score_recall-marked.txt](funes/score_recall-marked.txt).
  On those spans, the `funes recall` pass ranks 446 questions as that run does. Each of the 24 others
  has a copy of a marked session among its spans, and 23 of them rank alike on the final gold. Two of
  them, asked three times each, show why: the copy's turn and the marked one get the same score, and
  they swap places from one run to the next.
- funes and `score_recall` were built from `huggingface/funes` at `29075cb`.

## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents:
  296,084 drawers from 18,468 files. It skips a conversation whose text it has already stored, so
  each of the other 727 files, a copy of a session it kept, is registered without a drawer. The
  `mcp-sweep` arm copies that palace and adds `mempalace sweep ~/.claude/projects/`, one drawer per
  record (199,499), copies included.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default
  limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU. Its
  server passed 22 long questions through its query sanitizer, which shortened 6 of them (one from 355
  characters to 83).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's own normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript
  equals `normalize_conversations` for 18,416 of the 18,468 files. The 52 others are the sessions that
  hold only assistant turns: none holds a span, and no passage came from them. 295,940 of 295,940
  chunks of the 18,416 files equal their stored drawer. A drawer is the run of turns its text covers:
  median 2 turns per passage returned in `mcp`, at most 4. All 3,760 passages of each arm were placed
  this way.

## agentmemory 0.9.31

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine v0.25.0 its CLI pins.
  `agentmemory import-jsonl` reads 200 files by default, 1,000 at most with `--max-files`, and only
  under `~/.claude/projects`. So the driver moved the files into 20 subdirectories there, of up to
  1,000 files each, and imported each with `--max-files 1000`: 19,195 sessions, 199,499 observations,
  one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`: `memory_smart_search` and `memory_recall`, at their default 10
  results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a
  hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 4,700 of 4,700 passages.

## deja-vu 0.23.5

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 19,195 sessions, 199,499 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the
  hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (7,037 passages), else by its text in
  the session (44, and 3 of those in several turns, placed at the first). One was not found and counts
  for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its
  page shows 2 to 5 sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names
  (4,486 at one turn, 49 at several, 52 not found and counted for the session row only).
- deja-vu's own [day-zero page](https://vshulcz.github.io/deja-vu/guide/day-zero.html) asks
  LongMemEval over the same pile of 19,195 sessions, and scores by session: the first 100 answerable
  questions (64 single-session-user, 36 multi-session), found when an answer session is among the
  first 50 results. Here its session row (all 470 questions, the first 8 distinct sessions) is 280
  (`search`).
