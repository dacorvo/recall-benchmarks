## MemPalace 3.11.0

- `mempalace --palace P mine ~/.claude/projects/ --mode convos`, the backfill MemPalace documents: 53,171 drawers from 634 of the 635 files.
- Asked through `mempalace-mcp --palace P`, `mempalace_search {"query": q, "limit": 8}` (its default limit is 5), other arguments at their defaults. Its default `minilm` embedder ran on the CPU, from Python 3.12.16 with the packages `uv` resolved on 2026-11-10 (in the bucket's run record).
- Placement: the driver replays MemPalace's normalizer and chunker on each file. Its transcript equals `normalize_conversations` for 630 of the 634 files with drawers, and 53,164 of their 53,164 drawers equal the stored text (6 once a NUL is read as U+FFFD, as the store keeps it). All 800 passages came from those drawers and were placed at the run of turns each covers: median 1 turn, at most 22.

## agentmemory 0.9.33

- Keyless (no LLM provider, no embeddings), with the iii engine 0.25.1 its CLI pins. `agentmemory` starts the server. `agentmemory import-jsonl` reads 200 files by default, so the driver imported each project directory in batches of up to 1,000 files with `--max-files 1000`: 2 imports, 635 files as 99,846 observations, one per content block.
- Asked through `agentmemory mcp`, the stdio MCP server an agent is wired to: `memory_smart_search` (the call its recall skill makes) and `memory_recall`, at their default 10 results. Both rank with BM25 alone when keyless and gave the same hits for every question.
- Placement: an observation keeps its record's timestamp, which places it at that turn. Its id, a hash of the record's uuid and the block's index, names the same turn for 1,000 of 1,000 passages.

## deja-vu 0.24.1

- `deja index --rebuild`: it reads 635 sessions, 58,573 messages.
- `search`: `deja search --json --limit 8 <q>`. A passage is one of a hit's snippets, found among the hit's messages by its text and placed at that message's time (500 passages), else by its text in the session (1,011, and 118 of those in several turns, placed at the first). 12 were not found and count for the session row only.
- `recall`: `deja recall <q>` with `DEJA_RECALL_GLOBAL=1`, run outside any project of the corpus. Its page shows sessions with excerpts, each placed by its text in the session the page names (310 at one turn, 37 at several, 5 not found and counted for the session row only).
- Its ranking includes a freshness term (`search.freshnessDecay` in the binary), so the same release asked on another day can score a session otherwise.
